In recent years, ML has been used in novel applications—from generating new art and music to systems that accurately predict outcomes of medical interventions in real-time. In this course, we introduce the foundational concepts behind the probabilistic paradigm of predictive ML: statistical model specification and learning, focusing on connecting theory with real-world applications. Students get hands-on experience building models for specific tasks, taken from healthcare contexts, using a probabilistic programming language, while introducing critical perspectives to examine the ethics of ML within sociotechnical systems.
