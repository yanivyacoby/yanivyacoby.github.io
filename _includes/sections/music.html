I've had the privilege of collaborating with numerous musicians in the Boston area, including fiddler Eric Boodman, baroque violin/nyckelharpa player Anna Breger, accordionist/singer Sunniva Brynnel, pianist Chase Morrin, ensembles Blue Thread and Fade Blue. In 2016, I released my debut album The Corn Knight, an hour long story piece for piano/marimba duo, in collaboration with Chase Morrin. While at the New England Conservatory, I had the privilege of studying with Carla Kihlstedt, Cristi Catt, Win Horan, Hankus Netsky, Eden MacAdam-Somer and Dan Bauch.
