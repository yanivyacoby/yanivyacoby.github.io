My research develops new machine learning and AI methods to advance the understanding, prediction, and prevention of suicide and related behaviors. In the [Model-Guided Uncertainty (MOGU) Lab](https://mogu-lab.github.io/), I build probabilistic models designed for safety-critical settings—methods that make the consequences of modeling assumptions and inference decisions transparent, so predictions can be interrogated and trusted. This work bridges ML methodology, human-computer interaction, and clinical psychology.
