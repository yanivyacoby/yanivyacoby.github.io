I lead the [Model-Guided Uncertainty (MOGU) Lab](https://mogu-lab.github.io/), where we develop ML/AI methods to better understand, predict, and prevent suicide. Much of what makes clinicians effective--and much of what patients understand about their own experience--is tacit and difficult to put into words, let alone to translate it into a predictive model. To address this, we build probabilistic (Bayesian) methods that elicit this hard-to-verbalize expertise from clinicians and patients and embed it directly into a model's structure and assumptions, rather than bolting it on after the fact. This enables us to better work with the sparse, noisy, and messy data often found in mental health studies.
